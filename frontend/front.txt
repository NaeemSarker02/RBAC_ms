RBAC Frontend – Premium UI Redesign
Complete visual overhaul of the React + Tailwind v4 + Framer Motion frontend. Goal: dark, glassy, premium aesthetic — professional identity with vibrant accents, micro-animations, and fully responsive layout.

Design Language:

Theme: Dark sidebar + glassmorphism cards + luminous indigo/violet accent palette
Typography: Inter (Google Fonts)
Colors: Deep slate backgrounds (#0f172a, #1e293b) + vibrant indigo/violet (#6366f1, #8b5cf6) accents
Elevation: layered shadows, glowing hover states, frosted-glass panels
Motion: Framer Motion page transitions, staggered card entries, hover scale pulses
